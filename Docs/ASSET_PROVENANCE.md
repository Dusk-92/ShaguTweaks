## Important licensing boundary

An exact upstream match establishes that these files were inherited unchanged
from ShaguTweaks.

It does **not** by itself establish whether every visual element was wholly
created by the upstream project, derived from World of Warcraft assets, or
otherwise subject to separate third-party rights.

No additional ownership or relicensing claim is made by this fork.

World of Warcraft, Warcraft, Blizzard Entertainment, and associated names,
marks, artwork, and game assets remain the property of their respective rights
holders.

## Maintenance

When an asset is added, removed, or replaced:

1. record its source or creation history;
2. record an exact upstream match when one can be verified;
3. do not guess unresolved provenance;
4. preserve historical attribution when replacing an inherited asset.
